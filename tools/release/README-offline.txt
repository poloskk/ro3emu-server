
This version: RO3Emu with fake players, by FroiCHoi
-------------------------------------------------
This build adds a realm of fake players (bots that log in, hunt, vend, trade,
join guilds and sieges, and fight in your party), the RO3EmuPlay launcher, and
crystal drops. Its source code, as the GPL requires (see LICENSE.txt):
  https://github.com/poloskk/ro3emu-server/tree/feat/population
Based on RO3Emu by Hubert Kozlowski: https://github.com/aethai/ro3emu-server

Updates: RO3EmuPlay checks for a new version when it starts and shows an Update
button. Your characters, settings and game folder stay as they are.
